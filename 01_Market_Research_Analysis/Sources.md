## 1. India Market Context

### 1.1 Euromonitor International --- Sportswear in India

-   **Source:** Euromonitor International, *Sportswear in India* (public
    report overview)
-   **URL:** https://www.euromonitor.com/sportswear-in-india/report
-   **Used for:** Broad India sportswear market context, reported market
    growth, forecast direction, and category-level competitive context.
-   **Important figures cited in the research report:** The public
    overview reports 2025 retail sales of approximately **INR 824.1
    billion**, current-value growth of **11% in 2025**, and a forecast
    of approximately **INR 1.30 trillion by 2030**, representing a **10%
    CAGR**.
-   **Limitations:** These are broad sportswear-category estimates, not
    Nike-specific or Gen Z-specific sales. The full report and detailed
    methodology were not reviewed; figures are reproduced as presented
    in the public overview and should be rechecked against the latest
    edition before formal publication.

### 1.2 Hindustan Foods Limited --- Annual Report 2024--25

-   **Source:** Hindustan Foods Limited, Annual Report 2024--25,

    https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/annual_reports/AR_27657_HNDFDS_2024_2025_A_11429527_25082025180640.pdf
-   **Used for:** Secondary context on the India athletic-footwear

    report cites an estimate of approximately **USD 5.9 billion for
    CY2024**, with a forecast of approximately **USD 9.8 billion by
    CY2033** and a **5.2% CAGR**.
-   **Limitations:** This is a secondary citation within a company
    annual report, not a direct review of the underlying IMARC study.
    Athletic footwear is narrower than broad sportswear, so these
    estimates should not be added to or directly compared with the
    Euromonitor sportswear figures.

## 3. Research Design and Analytical Framework

The project uses a structured desk-research framework to organize the
opportunity assessment: - **Market context:** Broad market estimates are
used to describe category scale and direction, not to claim a precise
Nike-addressable market. - **Consumer segments:** Campus Trendsetters,
Fitness Enthusiasts, Young Urban Professionals, and Sneaker & Culture
Enthusiasts are working hypotheses for research segmentation. They are
not validated consumer clusters. - **Consumer needs:** Price/value,
lifestyle relevance, creator influence, product comfort, discovery, and
availability are treated as hypotheses to test, not established findings
about all Indian Gen Z consumers. - **Competitor analysis:** The
workbook compares broad positioning cues and provides an SKU-audit
structure. Directional scorecard values are analyst hypotheses, not
measured consumer ratings or a brand ranking. - **Strategic
implications:** Proposed growth opportunities are hypotheses generated
from the desk-research framework. They require validation through
primary research and company-specific economics.

No primary Gen Z survey, structured interview study, retail-store audit,
live size-level stock audit, or Nike internal-data analysis is claimed
in this version.

## 4. Data Quality, Limitations & Responsible Use

1.  **Market definitions vary.** Sportswear, sports apparel, and
    athletic footwear are distinct market categories. Their estimates
    should not be summed or compared as though they share one
    definition.
2.  **Market estimates are provider-specific.** Figures may differ
    because of scope, channel coverage, currency conversion, base year,
    and forecasting assumptions.
3.  **No Gen Z-specific market share is inferred.** Broad
    company/category figures are not evidence of brand preference or
    market share among Indian consumers aged 18--27.
4.  **SKU prices are indicative benchmarks.** Exact model variants,
    retailer prices, discounts, sizes, and availability may change. The
    workbook does not claim a live, date-stamped capture of each product
    listing.
5.  **Availability is out of scope.** Live size-level inventory was not
    independently assessed and is intentionally excluded from the
    scorecard.
6.  **Scorecard values are directional.** They represent structured
    hypotheses based on broad positioning cues, not survey results,
    audited performance metrics, or an objective ranking.
7.  **Financial outputs require assumptions.** Any conversion, average
    order value, margin, acquisition cost, or investment figures used in
    subsequent modelling must be clearly labelled as assumptions unless
    supported by verified data.
8.  **No company endorsement or internal access.** This is an
    independent case study based on public information and illustrative
    analysis.

### Recommended validation before making external claims

-   Recheck source editions, market definitions, and publication dates.
-   Capture exact competitor SKUs, prices, channels, and observation
    dates if product-level claims are needed.
-   Conduct consumer research with a clearly described sample and
    method.
-   Replace illustrative scorecard values with evidence-backed measures
    where data is available.
-   Label all financial scenarios and avoid presenting hypothetical
    outcomes as Nike forecasts.
