# Nike: Winning the Next Generation

## Gen Z Market Expansion Strategy | India

**An Independent Consulting Case Study by Tushar Verma**
PGDM – Big Data Analytics | Goa Institute of Management

## Project Overview

This project explores how Nike could strengthen its relevance and profitable growth among Indian Gen Z consumers through a differentiated, data-informed market expansion strategy.

The case study combines market research, competitor analysis, strategic recommendations and illustrative financial modelling to develop a proposed approach for engaging young Indian consumers.

## Business Objective

To identify potential opportunities for Nike to:

* Strengthen brand relevance among Indian Gen Z consumers.
* Build engagement through sports, culture and communities.
* Improve product discovery and purchase conversion.
* Develop a measurable and scalable market expansion strategy.

## Project Deliverables

1. **Market Research Analysis** – Market context, consumer hypotheses, working segments, trends and opportunity areas.
2. **Competitor Analysis** – Qualitative comparison of Nike and other sportswear brands, with directional hypotheses and illustrative price benchmarks.
3. **Financial Model** – Downside, base and upside scenarios, assumptions, sensitivity analysis and dashboard.
4. **Consulting Report** – Strategic framework, implementation roadmap, KPIs, risks and proposed next steps.
5. **Final Presentation** – Executive summary of the business problem, strategy and implementation approach.

## Strategic Framework

* **Youth-Culture Relevance:** Connect sport, style and everyday youth interests.
* **Community-Led Engagement:** Explore campus, creator and activity-based engagement.
* **Commerce Experience:** Reduce friction between product discovery and purchase.

## Methodology

The project uses public secondary research, qualitative competitor comparisons, working consumer hypotheses and illustrative financial assumptions. Primary research and pilot validation are proposed as next steps.

## Important Disclaimer

This is an independent, hypothetical portfolio case study. It is not commissioned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Nike. It does not use Nike's internal data, and its consumer segments, strategic hypotheses and financial assumptions have not been validated as actual Nike findings or forecasts.
